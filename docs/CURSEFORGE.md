# Shardline

**Shards have shattered the world. Step through the portal, face the Crystal Colossus and the Shard Queen – and deal with what you set free.**

Shardline is a story-driven dimension and boss mod for **Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge)**, built for the Shardline.eu network. Deep in the deepslate of the Overworld lies a rare ore: shards, fragments of a foreign world. Build a portal from shard frames and enter the **Shard Expanse**, a dark dimension of floating crystal islands over an endless void, with its own sky, fog and eternal night.

## What you get

**A new dimension**
- The Shard Expanse: three biomes (Crystal Forest, Ash Fields, Glowing Caves), floating islands, shattered moon, custom atmosphere
- Portal built from Shard Frames and lit with a Shard Key, scaled 4:1 to the Overworld

**Creatures and bosses**
- 11 new creatures: Shard Creeper, Crystal Golem, Glow Moth (tameable), Ash Walker, Shard Scout, Void Ray (rideable), Crystal Spider, Shadow Fox, Shattered Knight, Crystal Snail and the Shard Trader
- 5 bosses with phases and their own boss bars: **Crystal Colossus**, **Shard Queen**, **Virus Heart**, **Echo** and **Festering Heart**
- All animated with GeckoLib

**Gear and crafting**
- Shard armor and tools, Colossus armor (smithing upgrade with 4 bonus hearts and the Crystal Shield on **V**)
- Crystal Bow, Colossus Gauntlet, Heart of the Expanse (void blink), Queen's Shardblade
- Crystal wood set, polished shardstone, shard lantern, crystal glass, glow food, Resonance Compass
- Enchantments: Shard Bane and Crystal Ward

**11 structures**
Shard Crater, Ruin Tower, Keeper Shrine, Trader Outpost, Ash Crypt, Crystal Geode, Keeper Ruins, Survivor Camp, Keeper Library, Colossus Arena and the Queen's Throne.

## The story: 9 chapters

Long ago the Expanse was **Aurelis**, one endless crystal held together by a song. A false note, the **Shardvirus**, began to spread. Selvaine, the last Keeper, took it into herself and became the seal. Centuries later she is the glass queen guarding the throne.

Kill her, and the seal breaks.

- **Chapters 1–6:** discover the shards, find 12 lore books and 8 memory visions, defeat the Colossus, break the seal and contain the outbreak with Purity Anchors, Purification Essence and three Virus Hearts
- **The decision:** seal the virus or rule it. Server mode: vote with all players or first player decides. Each path has its own title, rewards and abilities
- **Chapters 7–9:** a survivor camp, NPCs with full dialogue, 10 quests, a quest journal (**J**), festering nodes, two different final battles and four epilogues

## The Shardvirus

After the Queen falls, the virus spreads through blocks, animals and villagers. It creeps into stone, into the Nether (**Emberblight**) and the End (**Voidrot**), turns cows, sheep, pigs, villagers, piglins and endermen into crystallized creatures, and causes Shard Fever. Fight back with Purity Anchors that grow a protection zone, cure villagers and become immune.

Performance is built in: spreading runs through a per-chunk queue with a per-tick limit, never loads chunks, and can be paused or reset with commands.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.21.1**, **NeoForge** 21.1.264 or newer
- **GeckoLib** 4.9.3 or newer (required)
- JEI or EMI (optional, shows recipes and info pages)
- **Install on the server and on every client.** A Paper or Spigot server will not work.

## Server friendly

- Everything runs on the server, the client only displays
- Extensive config: spawn rates, boss health and damage, set bonuses, virus speed and depth, dimensions, ending mode, abilities, dialogue and quests
- Dialogues and quests are datapack-driven and can be edited or extended
- Commands for operators: `/shardline story`, `/shardline virus`, `/shardline quest`, `/shardline lore`
- Existing worlds keep working. The ore appears only in newly generated chunks
